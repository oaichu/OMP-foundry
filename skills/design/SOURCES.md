# Design knowledge provenance

OMP Foundry's design skills are original, distilled control-plane guidance. The upstream projects below are not vendored and are not runtime dependencies.

Reviewed 2026-08-27:

- `nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill` (MIT): visual-language and multi-platform design-intelligence inspiration.
- `pbakaus/impeccable` (Apache-2.0): critique, accessibility, interaction, motion, and visual-QA inspiration.
- `ibelick/ui-skills` (MIT): durable design-contract and context-routing inspiration.

Foundry policy: keep one owner per capability, preserve progressive/JIT loading, and import upstream ideas only through deliberate review. Do not auto-sync external skill corpora into the package.
